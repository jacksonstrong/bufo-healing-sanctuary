Safety comes first

Sacred medicine deserves deep respect.

Too many people walk into these medicines rushed and unprepared. Here, nothing is rushed. Not every medicine is right for every person. If it isn't right for you yet, Marla will tell you honestly and help you find another path.

Every guest completes a medical intake. Iboga guests also complete an ECG and lab work.

We review every medication and supplement for possible interactions, together with your doctor.

Where is the sanctuary?

We're nestled in the East Piney Woods of Texas, among trees, wildlife, and quiet places to reflect. We're within reach of Houston, Dallas, and Austin. Exact location details are shared with confirmed guests.

How do I reserve a ceremony or retreat?

It starts with a private Discovery Call. Together you'll explore your intentions and your options. If you choose to continue, we guide you through intake, health screening, preparation, and scheduling. Choosing this path should be a calm, informed decision, made without pressure.

What if I'm not a good fit for a medicine?

Not every medicine is right for every person, and timing matters. If screening shows a concern, we may suggest more medical review, waiting, or a different approach. We also offer preparation and integration work that doesn't involve ceremony at all.

What should I bring?

You'll receive a full preparation guide before you arrive. Usually that means comfortable clothes, personal essentials, and anything that helps you rest. A journal is welcome but never required.

Are donations accepted?

Yes. As a 501(c)(3) spiritual sanctuary, donations support our mission, our education, and our scholarship fund for veterans and first responders. Contributions for specific ceremonies and retreats are shared during your Discovery Call.
