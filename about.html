Our foundation

Sacred medicine is a relationship.

It is built through preparation, integrity, embodiment, responsibility, and integration. The ceremony is one part of the journey. How you carry what is revealed into your life is just as sacred.

Our foundation lives where ancient wisdom, ceremonial stewardship, nervous system awareness, and spiritual growth meet. Every guest is welcomed into a process that values safety, education, discernment, ethical stewardship, personal responsibility, and ongoing integration support.

We see sacred medicines as sacred catalysts. When approached consciously and with respect, they may support deeper self-awareness, healing, remembrance, spiritual awakening, and transformation. They are never a shortcut or a quick fix.

Our spiritual path

Our path is rooted in a direct, personal relationship with God, Great Spirit, the Divine, and Sacred Mother Earth. We honor the sovereignty of each person. Your relationship with the Divine is your own, and it does not require any traditional or man-made religious doctrine.

We approach ceremony as a spiritual practice, with reverence, intention, prayer, preparation, and respect for the sacrament.

Spiritual freedom & sacred practice

Protected by our freedom of faith.

Our sincere spiritual beliefs, ceremonies, and sacramental practices exist within the legal framework of religious freedom in the United States.

The First Amendment protects the free exercise of faith. The Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA) protects sincere religious exercise from substantial government burden, under the standards set by that law.

The law uses the words "religion" and "religious exercise." We describe our own path as deeply spiritual. We honor these protections while staying rooted in a direct relationship with the Divine, sacred ceremony, spiritual sovereignty, and sincerely held beliefs.

Our ceremonies are offered with reverence, integrity, responsibility, careful stewardship, and respect for applicable law.

Statement of beliefs

What we hold sacred.

We intend to support the transformation and evolution of people seeking enlightenment in communion with the Divine Creator, through the ceremonial and sacred use of natural entheogens. To fulfill this purpose, we organize retreats and sacred ceremonies for the expansion of consciousness, along with other guided relaxation practices and practices of ancestral consciousness.

We believe natural entheogens were manifested by the Divine Creator so that all people, regardless of religion, race, sex, or gender, may commune with the Divine.
We believe the ceremonial and sacred use of natural entheogens helps our members access their divinity from within.
We believe, from our experience, that sacramental use within an appropriate spiritual context benefits the expansion of consciousness and supports each person's spiritual quest.
We believe our love and connection to all things deepens through sacrament. It often brings mystical experiences of higher spiritual realms, where we meet the Divine Creator and other spiritual beings.
We believe mystical experiences bring answers and guidance to life's deepest questions, and unite us through love, understanding, and connection with all beings.
We believe the overarching message we receive is that unconditional love is one of the most powerful forces in the universe, and the very nature of the Divine Creator.
We believe sacrament restores a more symbiotic relationship with Mother Nature, and so we are called to respect and preserve her, and to encourage others to do the same.
We believe sacramental use of entheogens is a very ancient practice, perhaps over 100,000 years old. Ayahuasca and San Pedro ceremonies date back more than 2,000 years. We seek to honor the ancestors in how we practice.
We believe that at death our spirits return to the same realm we touch through sacrament.
We believe becoming at home in the spiritual realm helps us overcome the fear of death, so we can focus on loving others and making the universe better for all living beings.
We believe our members carry a moral and ethical duty to meet every situation with peace, love, and understanding.
We believe other sacred texts and teachings, where they are consistent with the Divine Creator, offer guidance in how to live, love, and be tolerant of all beings.

An indigenous prophecy of the Americas

The Eagle & the Condor.

When the Eagle of the North and the Condor of the South fly together in the same sky, the spirit of the land will reawaken.

August 13, 1521The Spanish conquest of the Aztec civilization
500 yearsA time of healing, recovery, and reconnection
August 13, 2021The prophecy's 500-year span completes

The medicines of North and South

In this telling, Grandfather Peyote, a cactus of the North American deserts, is the Eagle of the North. Grandmother Ayahuasca, a sacred brew of the Amazon, is the Condor of the South. The shared sky is understood as the Sonoran Desert.

The Sonoran Desert is home to the Bufo Alvarius toad. It lives deep in the earth for nine months of the year and rises to the surface for only three. August is the peak month for gathering its medicine. In this way, the toad and its medicine are seen as the reawakening of the spirit of the land.

A secret kept for 500 years

One line of thought holds that the Aztecs foresaw the end of their civilization. In response, they hid many of their artifacts and oral teachings, including their knowledge of the sacred Bufo Alvarius medicine. There is a historical link between the toad and the Aztecs. Its spiritual role has only been widely recognized in recent times.

We understand this hiding as protection, for the medicine itself and for humanity, until it could be rediscovered now. The Aztecs held deep knowledge of many things, including astrology and their careful calendars.

Interpretations of this prophecy differ among indigenous communities and individuals, and hidden histories are hard to prove. We hold it with respect and humility. We ask everyone who carries this medicine to keep learning, together, from diverse cultures about the true history of Bufo Alvarius.
