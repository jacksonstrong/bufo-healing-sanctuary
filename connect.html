Reservations & General Information

Where is Bufo Healing Sanctuary located?

Bufo Healing Sanctuary is nestled in the beautiful East Piney Woods of Texas, surrounded by nature, trees, wildlife, and peaceful spaces for reflection and connection. Our private sanctuary provides a nature-centered environment for sacred medicine education, personal exploration, ceremonies, retreats, and integration experiences. Specific location and arrival information are shared with confirmed guests.

How do I reserve a ceremony or retreat?

The first step is to schedule a private Discovery Call. During our conversation, we explore your intentions, answer your questions, and discuss the different ceremony and retreat options. If you wish to continue, we guide you through the intake and waiver process, health screening, preparation requirements, and scheduling. We believe that choosing to participate should be a conscious, informed decision without pressure.

How soon will I hear back?

Marlo replies within 48 hours.

Are donations accepted?

Yes. Bufo Healing Sanctuary welcomes donations in support of our spiritual and educational mission. Donations help support the sanctuary's continued development, educational resources, community initiatives, and opportunities to make our work accessible to others. Information about contributions associated with specific retreats, ceremonies, or training programs is shared during the inquiry process.

What should I bring to my retreat?

Before your retreat, we provide preparation guidance and information about what to bring. Depending on the experience, this may include comfortable clothing, personal essentials, and items that support relaxation, reflection, and connection with nature. You are also welcome to bring a journal if writing resonates with you, but journaling is never required. Our intention is to create an environment where you can feel comfortable being yourself, without pressure or expectations about how your experience should unfold.

What happens if I am not an appropriate candidate for a medicine experience?

Not every sacred medicine is appropriate for every individual, and we honor that each person has their own needs, circumstances, and timing. If our screening process identifies concerns, we may recommend additional medical evaluation, postponing participation, or exploring a different approach. We also offer educational, preparation, and integration experiences that do not require participation in sacred medicine ceremonies. We believe there are many pathways to awareness, connection, and meaningful personal transformation.

Preparation, Screening & Safety

Why is preparation important?

Preparation is an opportunity to begin understanding yourself more deeply before entering a sacred medicine experience. It invites you to explore your intentions, emotional patterns, personal beliefs, conditioning, expectations, and the ways you respond to everyday life. At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, preparation includes education about the medicines, personal reflection, nervous system awareness, and the importance of informed choices. We believe meaningful personal transformation can begin through awareness, long before any ceremonial experience.

Do you offer preparation and pre-integration education?

Yes. Our four-week Preparation & Pre-Integration Course is designed to help participants develop greater awareness and understanding before entering a sacred medicine experience. The course explores conscious preparation of the mind, body, and soul, including:

  • Recognizing unconscious patterns and conditioning
  • Emotional awareness and personal responsibility
  • Neuroscience and sacred medicine education
  • Nervous system awareness and embodiment
  • Spiritual teachings and conscious reflection
  • Intention-setting and integration practices

Our intention is to encourage participants to become more conscious observers of their thoughts, emotions, beliefs, and everyday choices. This educational process complements, but does not replace, appropriate medical screening.

What medical screening is required?

Medical screening is individualized according to the sacred medicine being considered and each participant's health history. Our process begins with a comprehensive intake and medication review. Depending on the medicine and individual circumstances, additional evaluations may include an ECG/EKG, laboratory testing, or consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Iboga TA requires particular attention to cardiovascular health and appropriate clinical monitoring because of ibogaine's effects on heart rhythm. We approach screening as an important part of informed participation and personal well-being.

Can medications or medical conditions prevent participation?

Yes. Certain medications, medical conditions, and personal health considerations may make a particular sacred medicine experience inappropriate. Every medicine has unique properties, and each person responds differently. Our screening process helps identify potential contraindications and determine whether additional medical guidance is necessary. We encourage participants to be open about their health history and to work with their prescribing healthcare professional regarding any medication-related questions or changes.

What safety and professional resources are available?

At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, safety, preparation, education, and personal responsibility are foundational to our approach. Our sanctuary is equipped with an Automated External Defibrillator (AED), and Marla Lindner is trained and certified in CPR, First Aid, and AED use. We also collaborate with experienced professionals who provide CPR, First Aid, AED, and emergency response education. Our professional network includes experienced ibogaine specialists, neurological and neuroscience researchers, sacred medicine educators, and integration professionals. These connections allow us to continue expanding our knowledge through traditional wisdom, firsthand experience, and evolving scientific understanding. Medical professionals are not routinely onsite. Additional medical support may be arranged when available. For ibogaine-containing experiences, AED availability and CPR training are important emergency resources but do not replace qualified medical oversight, continuous cardiac monitoring, and appropriate emergency capabilities. Our commitment is to approach each individual with respect, thoughtful preparation, informed consent, and continued education.

Why aren't your ceremonial protocols and dosages published?

At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, we recognize that every individual is unique. Each person brings their own health history, sensitivity, previous experiences, intentions, and personal considerations into a sacred medicine journey. Our approach emphasizes individualized assessment rather than a one-size-fits-all experience. We carefully document and track the medicine, amounts administered, and each participant's responses throughout their ceremonial experience. These records are an important part of our commitment to responsible practices, continuity of care, and informed decision-making. While we maintain detailed individual records, our ceremonial protocols and dosage practices are not publicly shared or intended for independent replication. We believe responsible facilitation involves thoughtful preparation, comprehensive screening, careful documentation, informed consent, and integration, while recognizing that individualized dosing and monitoring cannot eliminate the inherent risks of sacred medicines.

Sacred Medicine

What is Bufo Alvarius (5-MeO-DMT)?

Bufo Alvarius, also known as the Sonoran Desert toad, produces a secretion containing 5-MeO-DMT, a powerful psychedelic compound. Experiences can involve profound changes in perception, awareness, and the sense of self. Its effects can be intense and unpredictable, making medical screening, informed consent, and appropriate safeguards essential.

What is Huachuma (San Pedro)?

Huachuma is a traditional Andean cactus medicine containing mescaline. It has a long history of ceremonial use and is often associated with emotional openness, connection to nature, reflection, and expanded awareness. Experiences may last many hours and can involve significant physical and psychological effects.

What is Iboga Total Alkaloid (Iboga TA)?

Iboga TA is an extract containing multiple naturally occurring alkaloids from the iboga plant, including ibogaine. It is associated with prolonged introspective experiences and is being studied for potential applications involving addiction and neurological processes. Ibogaine can cause life-threatening cardiac complications and requires specialized medical evaluation and monitoring.

What is the difference between Iboga TA and Ibogaine HCl?

Iboga TA contains a mixture of naturally occurring iboga alkaloids, whereas ibogaine hydrochloride (HCl) is a purified form of ibogaine. The two preparations differ in composition, and their effects and risks cannot be assumed to be equivalent. Neither preparation is established as inherently safer.

How do the three sacred medicines differ?

Bufo Alvarius is associated with a rapid, intense alteration of consciousness. Huachuma generally produces a longer experience that participants may describe as reflective and heart-centered. Iboga TA is associated with prolonged introspection and complex neurological effects. Each medicine has distinct pharmacological properties, contraindications, and potential risks.

Do I need sacred medicine to experience personal transformation?

Absolutely not. Personal growth, spiritual exploration, and greater self-awareness can develop through meditation, breath awareness, nature connection, reflection, conscious living, and many other practices. Sacred medicines are not necessary for meaningful transformation.

Ceremony & Retreat Process

How do I begin my journey with Bufo Healing Sanctuary?

Every journey begins with a private Discovery Call. This is an opportunity for us to connect, explore what is calling you toward sacred medicine, discuss your intentions, and answer your questions. We believe that understanding the individual is an important part of creating a thoughtful and personalized experience. From there, we explain our intake, screening, preparation, ceremony, and integration process.

What happens during a Discovery Call?

Our Discovery Call is a personal conversation where we explore your intentions, experiences, questions, and what you hope to understand or receive from your journey. We also introduce our different sacred medicine pathways and discuss the importance of preparation, emotional awareness, personal responsibility, and integration. There is no pressure or obligation. Our intention is to provide information and help you make a conscious, informed decision about your next steps.

What is involved in the intake and waiver process?

Following the Discovery Call, participants complete our intake questionnaire, health history, and informed consent and waiver documents. These forms help us better understand each individual, including their physical and emotional well-being, medications, previous experiences, and personal considerations. We review this information as part of our screening process and may request additional medical evaluation when appropriate. Honesty, transparency, and open communication are essential throughout this process.

What is the difference between private and group ceremonies?

Private ceremonies offer a more individualized experience, with dedicated attention to your personal intentions, preparation, and integration. Group ceremonies provide an opportunity to share a sacred space with others while honoring each participant's unique experience, boundaries, and personal journey. Both approaches emphasize intentional preparation, respectful guidance, personal sovereignty, and integration. We can discuss which environment may be most appropriate for you during your Discovery Call.

What retreat experiences are available?

Bufo Healing Sanctuary offers several sacred medicine and personal transformation pathways, including:

  • Private and group Bufo Alvarius ceremonies
  • Huachuma ceremonies and overnight retreats
  • Huachuma and Bufo retreat experiences
  • Iboga TA retreats
  • Three-Medicine Sacred Ceremony Journeys
  • Family and private group retreats
  • Preparation, integration, and spiritual education experiences

Each retreat is approached individually, with consideration for personal intentions, preparation, screening, and the nature of the experience.

What is the Seven-Day Three-Medicine Journey?

Our Seven-Day Three-Medicine Journey is a personalized sacred medicine retreat incorporating Huachuma, Iboga Total Alkaloid (Iboga TA), and Bufo Alvarius (5-MeO-DMT). Each medicine is approached through its own separate ceremony. We do not administer all three medicines on the same day. Every participant is unique, and the amount of medicine considered may vary based on individual health considerations, previous experiences, personal intentions, sensitivity, and other relevant factors. Some experiences may involve smaller, microdose-level amounts, while others may be more immersive. Microdosing does not eliminate the need for appropriate screening and medical safeguards. Between ceremonies, we incorporate dedicated grace days for rest, nourishment, nervous system awareness, personal reflection, and guided integration. These periods provide opportunities to process experiences and explore the emotions, insights, and awareness that may emerge. Our approach emphasizes comprehensive screening, intentional preparation, informed consent, and ongoing consideration of each participant's physical and emotional well-being. Because the medicines have distinct effects and potential interactions, individualized amounts and grace days do not establish that combining them is medically safe. Participants complete our four-week Preparation & Pre-Integration Course before their retreat, followed by four weeks of guided post-integration. At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, we believe the journey is not simply about the medicines themselves. It is about honoring each individual's unique process, allowing space for integration, and bringing greater awareness and conscious transformation into everyday life.

Can family members participate in retreats together?

Yes. We offer family-centered retreat experiences that create opportunities for connection, reflection, communication, and greater awareness within relationships. Families may explore personal patterns, emotional conditioning, boundaries, and ways of relating to one another with greater compassion and understanding. Each participant is considered individually, and participation in any medicine experience depends on appropriate screening, informed consent, and personal suitability. Family retreats may also incorporate non-medicine practices focused on connection, reflection, and integration.

Microdosing

What is microdosing?

Microdosing generally refers to taking a small amount of a psychoactive substance, typically below the level associated with a full psychedelic experience. Research into potential benefits remains limited and mixed, and even small amounts can carry medical, psychological, and medication-interaction risks.

What is the difference between microdosing and a full ceremonial experience?

Microdosing involves smaller amounts of sacred medicine, often explored as an ongoing practice to support emotional awareness, personal reflection, integration, and continued connection with the medicine without entering a full psychedelic experience. Through our firsthand experiences at Bufo Healing Sanctuary, we have observed participants describing meaningful benefits from their microdosing practices, including greater self-awareness, emotional connection, and a deeper appreciation for everyday life. Each person's experience is unique. A full ceremonial experience offers a more immersive exploration of consciousness, often inviting profound emotional, spiritual, and personal insights. Our approach honors both traditional wisdom and lived experience while recognizing the importance of preparation, individualized health screening, and integration. Bufo Alvarius is offered only within facilitated ceremonies, not for at-home microdosing. Iboga TA requires particular cardiovascular precautions, even at smaller amounts.

Are all sacred medicines appropriate for microdosing?

At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, our microdosing education focuses on Iboga Total Alkaloid (Iboga TA), Huachuma (mescaline), and Bufo Alvarius (5-MeO-DMT). Iboga TA and Huachuma may be explored through individualized microdosing practices, with an emphasis on education, preparation, health screening, and integration. Bufo Alvarius is approached differently. Smaller-dose experiences may be offered within a facilitated ceremony, but we do not provide Bufo for at-home microdosing. Each medicine has unique properties, and individual responses vary. Our approach emphasizes informed choices, personal awareness, and appropriate safety considerations, particularly cardiac screening for Ibogaine-containing preparations.

Can microdosing interact with medications or medical conditions?

Yes. Even smaller amounts of sacred medicine can interact with medications, supplements, and certain medical conditions. Our approach begins with understanding the individual, including their health history, current medications, personal intentions, and overall well-being. We encourage open communication, appropriate screening, and consultation with qualified healthcare professionals when needed. Iboga TA requires particular attention to cardiovascular health.

What is the relationship between microdosing and integration?

Microdosing and integration can complement one another, but they are not the same practice. Some individuals explore microdosing as a way to continue engaging with the intentions, reflections, and personal insights that emerged during a previous ceremonial experience. Integration is the conscious process of bringing those insights into everyday life through awareness, emotional reflection, personal responsibility, and intentional choices. At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, we believe the deeper work is not simply about the medicine itself, but about how we choose to live, grow, and embody what we are learning.

Integration & Personal Transformation

What is psychedelic integration?

Psychedelic integration is the conscious process of reflecting on an experience and exploring how its insights may relate to everyday life. Integration may involve recognizing unconscious patterns, understanding emotional responses, exploring personal beliefs, strengthening boundaries, and becoming more aware of the choices we make. At Bufo Healing Sanctuary, we believe integration is not simply about understanding what happened during a ceremony. It is about learning how to live with greater awareness, compassion, authenticity, and personal responsibility.

Why is integration important?

A profound experience can offer new perspectives, but meaningful change often develops through the choices we make afterward. Integration provides an opportunity to reflect on what we have experienced, recognize patterns that may no longer serve us, and explore new ways of responding to life. It is a process of becoming more conscious of our thoughts, emotions, relationships, and behaviors. We believe integration is an ongoing practice of awareness, embodiment, and conscious living rather than something that ends after a ceremony.

What integration support does Bufo Healing Sanctuary offer?

Our integration approach recognizes the connection between the mind, body, emotions, nature, and personal awareness. Depending on the individual and retreat experience, our offerings may include:

  • Personal Reflection & Soul Integration
  • Somatic Awareness & Embodiment
  • Gentle Flow Yoga & Mindful Movement
  • Nature & Water Integration
  • Animal Connection & Homestead Experiences
  • Crystal Sound Bowl Experiences
  • Frequency & Energy-Based Wellness Practices
  • Touch & Bodywork, including massage

These practices offer different opportunities for grounding, reflection, connection, and embodiment. Participants are encouraged to explore what resonates with them. All reflection and journaling exercises are optional.

Do you offer post-retreat integration?

Yes. We believe continued integration is an important part of the overall journey. Our extended retreat pathways may include four weeks of guided post-integration, offering opportunities for reflection, emotional awareness, embodiment, and conscious personal growth. During this time, participants can explore how their experiences relate to relationships, personal patterns, beliefs, boundaries, and everyday choices. Our intention is to encourage meaningful reflection and help individuals discover their own ways of integrating what they have experienced.

Can I receive integration support without attending a ceremony?

Absolutely. You do not need to participate in a psychedelic or sacred medicine ceremony to explore personal awareness, emotional patterns, spiritual connection, or conscious transformation. Our preparation and integration teachings can also support individuals navigating life transitions, relationships, grief, personal growth, or a desire to understand themselves more deeply. Awareness, reflection, embodiment, and conscious choice are practices available to everyone.

Facilitator Training & Education

Does Bufo Healing Sanctuary offer facilitator training?

Yes. Bufo Healing Sanctuary offers comprehensive sacred medicine facilitator training and educational pathways for individuals who feel called to deepen their understanding of ceremonial traditions, preparation, integration, and the responsibilities of holding space for others. Our programs combine experiential learning, spiritual teachings, neuroscience education, ethical awareness, safety education, and mentorship. We emphasize that becoming a facilitator involves much more than learning about a medicine. It requires personal awareness, responsibility, discernment, and an ongoing commitment to learning.

What facilitator training pathways are available?
  • Bufo Alvarius Facilitator Training
  • Huachuma Facilitator Training, Path of the Open Heart
  • Iboga TA Facilitator Training
  • Complete

Facilitator Pathway, All Three Sacred Medicines Our programs combine online education, in-person immersion, practical learning, and mentorship, with different time commitments depending on the pathway. The Complete Facilitator Pathway includes approximately 25 days of in-person immersion, online training, and a year-long structured mentorship.

What is included in facilitator training?

Our training pathways offer comprehensive educational resources and practical learning opportunities. Depending on the program, participants may receive education and resources involving:

  • Preparation, discovery calls, and integration
  • Screening awareness and informed consent
  • Neuroscience and sacred medicine education
  • Ceremony, embodiment, and space-holding responsibilities
  • Ethical boundaries and participant care
  • CPR, First Aid, AED, and emergency response education
  • Mentorship and supervised learning
  • Educational manuals, presentations, and templates
  • Intake forms, waivers, and professional resources
  • Practice development and organizational guidance
  • Spiritual teachings and conscious awareness

Our structured mentorship extends beyond graduation through continued connection, education, and community.

What does Bufo Healing Sanctuary certification represent?

Certification through Bufo Healing Sanctuary represents completion of our private spiritual and educational training requirements within our sanctuary community. It acknowledges participation in the applicable educational program, practical learning experiences, and mentorship expectations. Our certification is not a medical, psychological, therapeutic, or government-issued professional license and does not independently authorize anyone to administer controlled substances or practice a regulated profession. We encourage graduates to continue learning, honor professional boundaries, and understand the laws and responsibilities relevant to their work.
