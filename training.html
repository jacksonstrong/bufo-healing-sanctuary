Have you felt the call?

A path that begins with your own healing.

Maybe you have felt called to sacred medicine. First to receive, and one day to serve. This training is for you.

It is an intimate, experiential training that takes time and patience. It is rooted in integrity, presence, conscious preparation, nervous system awareness, and deep reverence for this work. You begin with your own transformation and healing. Then, step by step, you learn to support others with compassion and responsibility.

You move through each phase as you show readiness. Each step honors safety, ethics, and your own integration.

Phase 1

Receive

Receive sacred medicine in a safe, guided setting, and integrate your own experience.

Phase 2

Self-Serve

Grow your self-awareness and self-responsibility. Learn how to hold yourself first.

Phase 3

Support

Observe experienced facilitators. Assist with preparation, guest care, and stewardship under supervision.

Phase 4

Facilitate

Practice supervised facilitation with continued mentorship, reflection, and integration.

Iboga Total Alkaloid (TA) track

“Iboga is a sacred teacher of truth, structure, reflection, and deep inner awareness.” 14 days in person, about 120 to 140 experiential hours.

  • Introductions to Bufo Alvarius and Huachuma, when appropriate, with recovery time between experiences

Continuing education and research

Becoming a facilitator is the beginning of a lifelong commitment to learning. As research grows, so does our understanding of these medicines: their benefits, limits, contraindications, and responsibilities. Students are encouraged to keep learning with curiosity, discernment, and humility.

Safety first, always

Prepared for every moment.

Responsible facilitation means knowing participant safety, ethical boundaries, informed consent, and emergency care. Students learn medical and emotional screening, contraindications, medication interactions, and when to call for medical help.

We are honored to work with Cynthia Stubbins, founder of Premium CPR. She teaches CPR, First Aid, AED, and emergency response in our trainings, and she also trains CPR instructors. Cynthia has experienced Bufo Alvarius, Huachuma, and Changa herself, so she understands the ceremonial space from the inside.

CPR, First Aid, and AED certification is included according to your track. We confirm the details before you enroll.

Certification

What your certificate means.

Bufo Healing Sanctuary, a spiritual sanctuary and educational organization, may award certification when you complete your track's education, experience, mentorship, and competency requirements.

It represents
  • Completion of a structured, immersive facilitator program
  • Ceremonial education, preparation, integration, and mentorship
  • Knowledge of sacred medicines, neuroscience, safety, and ethics
  • Commitment to responsibility, presence, and participant sovereignty
  • Training in individual and group facilitation and boundaries
  • Recognition within the Bufo Healing Sanctuary community
  • A foundation for ongoing learning and growth
It does not represent
  • A medical, healthcare, therapy, or counseling credential
  • A government-issued or state-recognized license or accreditation
  • Authorization to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition
  • Authorization to prescribe, possess, administer, or distribute controlled substances
  • A guarantee of legal permission to hold ceremonies
  • A substitute for medical care or emergency services
  • A guarantee of employment, affiliation, or independent practice

Facilitators work within the limits of their education, competence, and applicable law. Completing training does not by itself give legal authority to facilitate ceremonies with controlled substances. Facilitators must recognize when a licensed medical or mental health professional is needed.

Build your own sanctuary

Guidance for those called to lead.

If you feel called to start your own spiritual sanctuary or sacred service organization, we share foundational resources and templates. These cover your mission and purpose, your ethical foundation, and how to structure your work around your own vision and values.

If you resonate with our mission, you may also explore affiliation with Bufo Healing Sanctuary's spiritual sanctuary structure, by mutual agreement and with appropriate oversight.

This guidance is educational. It is not legal or tax advice. Certification does not automatically create affiliation, legal recognition, tax-exempt status, or permission to work with controlled substances.
