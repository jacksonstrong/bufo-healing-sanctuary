Sacred Service Scholarship Fund

Some people are called before they can afford it.

The Sacred Service Scholarship Fund offers partial scholarships to people who feel deeply called to healing or sacred service but don't yet have the means. It is funded by community donations.

Scholarships may go toward select retreats, private ceremonies, integration support, or facilitator training. Spots are limited, and every scholarship is by application.

We look for integrity, personal responsibility, commitment to preparation and integration, and a sincere devotion to sacred service.

The fund lives on reciprocity. Recipients help share the scholarship and the sanctuary's mission in their own communities, so the support keeps growing for the next person.

What recipients commit to

This work is deep and sacred, never casual or recreational. Because donors make it possible, recipients give back by sharing their journey to bring hope to others.

Attend every preparation session before ceremony.

Attend every integration session after ceremony.

Take an active part in your healing, before, during, and after.

Create and share your healing story in your own voice.

Share your experience on your own social media to bring awareness and hope.

Allow the sanctuary to share parts of your journey, testimonial, photos, or videos for education and inspiration.

Support the Vision

Help build the future Iboga sanctuary.

We dream of a nature-centered sanctuary rooted in the traditional wisdom of Iboga and its natural alkaloids.

It would offer a multi-tiered immersion: safe preparation, proper medical screening and oversight, somatic awareness, nervous system support, embodiment, and real integration. The whole journey of mind, body, and soul, far beyond a single ceremony.

It would be a place to move past old programming and inherited beliefs, and to remember your own sovereignty and inner wisdom.

This vision is rooted in authenticity, integrity, and service, with no plans for an empire. We welcome people who feel truly aligned with the mission and want to help our education, sanctuary, and community outreach grow.
