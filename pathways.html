Our main medicine · Bufo Alvarius · 5-MeO-DMT

The Pathway of Stillness.

Bufo is often described as a gentle or powerful melting of the ego. Many guests feel deep stillness, surrender, and oneness with Source. They often speak of clarity, heart expansion, and a feeling of coming home to the pure essence of the soul.

It works fast. When vaporized, effects begin within seconds, and the main experience is brief compared to most other sacred medicines. Private and group ceremonies are available.

What research suggests so far

  • It may bring rapid shifts in mood, perspective, and self-awareness.
  • It may ease symptoms of depression, anxiety, and stress in some people.
  • It may support trauma processing. One real-world study of military veterans reported meaningful drops in PTSD symptoms, with careful screening and support.
  • It may bring powerful states of unity, transcendence, and ego dissolution.
  • It may grow acceptance, emotional openness, and psychological flexibility.
  • It acts strongly on the serotonin system, especially the 5-HT1A receptor, and may affect nervous, hormone, and immune pathways.
  • Early lab research points to possible brain plasticity and antidepressant-like effects.

The research on 5-MeO-DMT is still young. Much of it is observational or early-stage. This work is not without risk, and it calls for careful screening and support.

Grandfather Huachuma · San Pedro · Mescaline

The Pathway of the Heart.

Huachuma is often experienced as heart-opening and grounding. It gently opens the heart, so you may feel compassion, inner peace, and real connection with your higher self, with others, and with the natural world.

It moves slowly, over many hours. That gives you time for stillness, reflection, nature, and a softening of the nervous system. Many guests call it gentle yet deeply profound.

Huachuma may especially support you if you

  • Find it hard to express your emotions
  • Are processing grief or loss
  • Struggle to receive support from others
  • Long to feel gratitude, love, and presence again

Huachuma also sharpens perception, physically, emotionally, and spiritually. It can open a doorway to insight, creative vision, and alignment with your soul's truth. Emerging research suggests possible benefits for emotional well-being, psychological flexibility, and lasting shifts in perspective.

Grandfather Huachuma & Great Spirit Bufo

A journey of love, clarity, and connection. Within our retreats, Huachuma often becomes a grounding, heart-centered bridge. With preparation and integration, the medicines may work together to support emotional healing, nervous system recalibration, and deeper reconnection to life.

Iboga TA · Ibogaine · Ibogaine Restoration

The Pathway of Clarity.

Iboga is deeply inward and clarifying. Many people describe it as a medicine of truth and inner inventory. It works with the subconscious and the nervous system. It may help quiet looping thoughts and bring clear awareness of old ancestral imprints, trauma loops, and habits.

In this expanded state, you may witness your life from a soul's view. Past experiences, future paths, and your own true responsibility can come into the light. Iboga retreats run three to seven days.

Iboga Total Alkaloid (TA) is a full-spectrum extract of the iboga plant. It contains ibogaine plus other natural iboga alkaloids. Most human research studies ibogaine and its metabolite, noribogaine. Direct research on full-spectrum TA is more limited.

How it works

A symphony across the brain

Most classic psychedelics act mainly on one serotonin receptor (5-HT2A). Ibogaine works across many systems at once: serotonin, dopamine, glutamate, opioid-related, and sigma pathways. It may also support the brain's ability to form new pathways.

Ibogaine tends to quiet the analytical left brain, so inner chatter softens. It tends to wake the intuitive right brain, so visions, symbols, and memories may arrive without words. People often say they "watched a movie of their life" or "talked with their higher self."

The integration window

Noribogaine keeps working

After ibogaine is processed by the liver, it becomes noribogaine. Noribogaine can stay active in the body for one to three months.

During that time it keeps shaping serotonin and dopamine, and it may lift mood, focus, and emotional processing. This creates an "integration window," a season that is ideal for therapy, new habits, and healing.

What Iboga may support

  • Interrupting addictive patterns and compulsive loops
  • Fewer cravings and a reset of habit-forming behavior
  • Much easier opioid withdrawal, in properly screened settings
  • Mood regulation and relief from depressive symptoms
  • Neuroplasticity and brain adaptation
  • Growth factors linked to repair and recovery (GDNF and BDNF, in animal studies)
  • Deep self-reflection, insight, and emotional processing
  • Lasting change, when paired with preparation and integration

Iboga TA is also being studied for possible support with PTSD, traumatic brain injury (TBI), addiction, depression, and anxiety.

Science highlights

  • Addiction and craving: a 2022 systematic review found ibogaine may reduce withdrawal and craving, and called for more rigorous trials.
  • Opioid withdrawal: case series and observational studies reported strong improvement in acute withdrawal, and fewer opioid problems later for some people.
  • Brain growth factors: lab studies found ibogaine changed GDNF and BDNF, two factors tied to recovery and resilience.
  • Many systems at once: reviews describe ibogaine and noribogaine acting across several brain signaling systems.

Huachuma · Iboga TA · Bufo Alvarius

The Three-Medicine Sacred Ceremony Journey.

A seven-day journey of awareness, remembrance, and conscious change. Three traditional medicines, each with its own gift, and grace days for rest and reflection between each one.

Because these medicines have different effects, contraindications, and possibly serious interactions, joining requires careful evaluation and proper medical safeguards. Every retreat is considered one person at a time.

Discovery Call

A private talk about your intentions, your history, and what is calling you. Ask anything and share your concerns.

Medical screening

A full health screening, including an ECG/EKG, lab work, and more tests when needed.

Medication & substance review

We review medications, supplements, and substance use for contraindications. Any medication change is made with your own doctor.

Four-week preparation course

Required for every guest. Prepare mind, body, and soul through emotional awareness, intention setting, noticing patterns, and nervous system awareness, using neuroscience and spiritual teaching. Finishing the course does not guarantee medical eligibility or remove risk.

Acceptance & scheduling

After screening and medical clearance, we review eligibility, informed consent, and retreat plans. Not everyone is a good candidate for a multi-medicine journey.

Your seven days

A private, supportive setting with grace days and close attention to your body and heart. Each ceremony is held on its own. Our protocols, sequencing, and methods are not published and are not meant to be copied.

Four weeks of integration

Guided post-integration to reflect, feel, and bring your insights into daily life, relationships, beliefs, and choices.

Our commitment to safety

Skilled people around you

We have access to experienced ibogaine specialists, neuroscience researchers, and experts in preparation and integration. We also work with professionals who teach CPR, First Aid, AED, and emergency response.

Medical professionals are not routinely onsite. Extra medical support may be requested and arranged when available. It must be confirmed in advance and is not guaranteed.

A commitment to yourself

Four weeks, seven days, four weeks

Four weeks of preparation, a seven-day retreat, and four weeks of guided integration. The medicines are only one part of the journey. What you bring in, and how you integrate after, matters deeply.

We ask every guest to come with openness, honesty, personal responsibility, and a willingness to take part fully.

Important safety information. Iboga TA, Huachuma, and Bufo Alvarius carry significant medical and psychological risks. Combining them within a short time may add risks that are not well understood, including serious heart complications and dangerous drug interactions. Screening, careful dosing, observation, preparation courses, and grace days do not remove these risks. Specialist advice, preparation, and CPR training do not replace clinical oversight, cardiac monitoring, and emergency medical care during ibogaine. Our ceremonial protocols are not published and should never be copied on your own.
